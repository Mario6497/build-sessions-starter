Molecule viewer

Energy plot

Click a point to see its structure. Ctrl+click marks several points. ← → step through points, Delete removes the marked points (or the current one). ΔE is relative to the lowest remaining point.

Saving writes the remaining points (with their energies in the comment lines) as a multi-frame .xyz. It opens in Molden, VMD and again here. The original file is never changed.

Structure

Drag to rotate, mouse wheel to zoom. Click 2, 3 or 4 atoms to measure a bond, angle or dihedral. Click empty space to clear.

Atom numbers are the same as in the Gaussian input (starting at 1). The measurement stays when you move to another point, so you can follow a bond through a scan.
